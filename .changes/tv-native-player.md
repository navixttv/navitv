Fire TV and Android TV now have a built-in player. Films and episodes open full screen in it and play MKV, HEVC / 4K and surround-sound files that the TV's browser engine could not, without VLC. It remembers where you stopped, and Left / Right on the remote skip 10 seconds. Live channels that cannot play in the guide view open in it automatically.
