highlight: English and Spanish content by default

Categories and M3U groups your provider tags for other countries or languages (like "FR |", "KU –" or names in another script) now start hidden, so only English (US, UK, EN) and Spanish (ES, Latino, MX) categories show by default. Home's Recently Added row and hero skip foreign-tagged titles too. Turn any category back on in Manage categories or the groups view.
