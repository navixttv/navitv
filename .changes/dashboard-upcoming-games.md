Home has a new "Upcoming games" row: games on now or in the next 24 hours from your sports channels' guide, live games first, each with its start time and channel. Pick the sports you want (football, basketball, boxing & UFC, soccer, baseball, hockey) the first time it appears, and change them any time with "Choose sports". The "Live sports" row now also appears on Fire TV, Android TV and in the browser.
